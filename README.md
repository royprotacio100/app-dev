# My Favorite Series: *Stranger Things*

![Status](https://img.shields.io/badge/Status-Completed-brightgreen)

## About the Show
**Stranger Things** is a science fiction horror drama series on **Netflix**, created by the Duffer Brothers. It premiered in 2016 and is set in the small town of **Hawkins, Indiana** during the 1980s.

## Quick Facts
| Detail | Info |
|--------|------|
| Platform | Netflix |
| Genre | Sci-fi, Horror, Drama |
| Setting | Hawkins, Indiana (1980s) |
| Creators | The Duffer Brothers |

## Main Characters
- **Eleven**: a girl with psychokinetic powers
- **Mike, Dustin, Lucas, and Will**: the group of friends at the center of the story
- **Max**: a skilled skateboarder who joins the group
- **Hopper**: the town police chief
- **Joyce Byers**: Will's determined mother

## Why I Like It
1. The *story* mixes mystery, horror, and friendship.
2. The *80s setting* has great music, fashion, and nostalgia.
3. The *characters* grow and become easy to care about.
4. The *Upside Down* is a creepy and creative idea.

## Favorite Quote
> "Friends don't lie."
